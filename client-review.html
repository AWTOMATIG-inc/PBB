Iconography & Tone

Iconography

Consistent stroke-based icon set (Lucide) used throughout — no emoji, no photography. Icons appear alongside services, contact details, and product specs.

Tone & Voice

  • Professional, industrial-equipment-dealer feel
  • Trustworthy and straightforward — not flashy
  • Real data presented plainly (no invented stats)

Page-by-Page Content

Home

/
  • Hero: tagline, headline, one-line summary, "Browse Generators" / "Contact Us" calls to action
  • Catalog-at-a-glance panel: live breakdown of models by power band
  • Brand strip: all featured brands (John Deere, Cummins, Ricardo, Perkins, Volvo Penta, Deutz, plus Caterpillar & Doosan)
  • "Why Power Bank Bangladesh" highlights
  • Featured models (one per brand) linking into Products
  • Our Services: Sell / Buy / Rental / Service
  • Closing call-to-action band

Products

/products
  • Full catalog — 98 generator models across 6 fully-specified brands
  • Filter by brand and by power band (Small / Medium / Large / Industrial)
  • Each card: brand, model, standby/prime kVA, engine, weight, fuel tank
  • Live result count and "clear filters" control; no pricing or checkout — a browsable spec sheet

About

/about  (next up)
  • Company story and trust messaging
  • The four services explained in more depth: what Sell, Buy, Rental, and Service each mean for a customer

Contact

/contact  (planned)

  • All phone numbers and the company email
  • Info only — no contact form, no online payment
